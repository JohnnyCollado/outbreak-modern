## Answer
Max per-channel delta from legitimate causes, 8-bit units:
- 16->8 expansion (c<<3 vs c<<3|c>>2): 7, uniform, value-dependent (delta = c>>2).
- DTHE dither on vs off (16-bit target): 8 with c<<3 expansion, 9 with bit replication; 4x4 periodic.
- COLCLAMP off vs on: wrap vs clamp, large (not +-1); treat as a bug, not tolerance.
- Alpha-blend >>7 truncation vs rounding variants: 1 per blend step, compounds, uniform-ish.
- CRTC merge/deinterlace (field vs frame, bob vs weave): large on moving content, line-alternating.
Suggested gate: d=9 per channel, p<=1% of pixels exceeding d, on static boot screens (after the bug-free path is confirmed). Calibrate with PCSX2 toggles before freezing.

## Findings
1. Expansion. Math is solid: c in 0..31; c<<3 vs (c<<3)|(c>>2) differ by c>>2, max 7 at c=31 (248 vs 255). PCSX2 expansion body NOT located (GSLocalMemory ReadPixel16 bodies not in fetched text; only m_psm[PSMCT16].fmsk = 0x80F8F8F8 seen). Which one PCSX2 uses: unverified.
2. Dithering. PCSX2 GSDrawScanline.cpp (pcsx2/GS/Renderers/SW/): dither only when sel.fpsm==2 && sel.dthe; row y=(top&3)<<1; per channel 16-bit add of global.dimx[] before the shift (rb>>3, ga>>6 style). Dither applies at draw time, baked into the stored 16-bit pixel, not at readout. Derived: dithered 5-bit = floor((c8+d)/8), d in -4..3, so 5-bit differs by at most 1 LSB -> 8 units (c<<3) or 9 (replication). Flat colour with 8-bit residue r=c mod 8: r=4 never changes; r in 0..3 can drop one level, r in 5..7 can gain one level, in some cells of the 4x4 pattern. DIMX values (-4..+3 pattern) recalled as {-4,0,-3,1},{2,-2,3,-1},{-3,1,-4,0},{3,-1,2,-2}; NOT verified against the manual.
   PCSX2 side: GSRendererSW::GetScanlineGlobalData sets sel.dthe from env.DTHE.DTHE and expands DIMX. No "dither setting" option found in fetched SW code.
3. CRTC/merge. PCSX2 GSRenderer::Merge(int field) (pcsx2/GS/Renderers/Common/GSRenderer.cpp): MMOD==1 && ALP==0 gates circuit-1 blending; background = BGCOLOR | (PMODE.ALP<<24); deinterlace via GSConfig.InterlaceMode and SMODE2.FFMD. GSState.cpp: isinterlaced() = SMODE2.INT; isReallyInterlaced() = (SYNCV.VFP&1) && SMODE1.CMOD. No 448-line calc found; 448 appears only in a comment. Effect: field vs frame vs deinterlace changes are line-alternating (bob/weave), can be large on motion, zero on static frames. Exact GS-manual text on FFMD/448 not retrieved.
4. Blend. Manual formula verified (ps2tek GS Alpha Blending): Output = (((A-B)*C)>>7)+D, COLCLAMP "8-bit signed clamp (0=8-bit AND)". PCSX2 SW blend: rb.sub16, rb.modulate16<1>(a), rb.add16 (modulate16 body not fetched). Truncation vs rounding: 1 LSB per blend, compounds. FBA: PCSX2 WriteFrame sets bit 31 when sel.fba && fpsm!=1 (alpha bit, not colour). Bilinear/UV precision: not researched.

Solid: expansion math, >>7 formula, DTHE gating and add-before-shift in PCSX2 SW, COLCLAMP AND masking, CRTC flags above.
Speculative: DIMX matrix values, PCSX2 expansion variant, exact spatial pattern of CRTC artifacts, bilinear deltas.

Gate form: d = max per-channel delta allowed per pixel; p = max fraction of pixels above d. Start d=9, p=0.01 for static screens; treat any |delta|>=16 as failure regardless of p.
